# IIC x ACE: The Idea Cafe (3-Hour Run of Show)

**Event:** The First Idea Cafe Meeting
**Attendees:** Students (Pitching), Teachers (Mentoring), 36 Coordinators (Managing)
**Time:** 3 Hours
**Convener:** B. Kalyan Reddy

Because you have 3 hours, you cannot just let students talk the whole time, or people will get bored. You need to structure this like a professional startup event. Here is your minute-by-minute guide:

---

## 🕒 HOUR 1: The Setup & The Vision (0:00 to 1:00)

**0:00 - 0:15 | The Convener's Opening Speech (You)**
*   You take the stage. Welcome everyone. 
*   **Crucial:** Do not talk about the college. Talk about the **Ministry of Education** and the **YUKTI Portal**. Tell the students: *"We are here today to find the best ideas to push to the national level for funding. Do not be afraid if your idea is basic. Today is about finding the spark."*

**0:15 - 1:00 | "Raw Pitches" (Round 1)**
*   Call up the registered students one by one. 
*   They get exactly **3 minutes** to explain their idea. (Assign one of your coordinators to hold a stopwatch and clap when 3 minutes are up).
*   Tell the teachers and audience to just listen. No harsh criticism yet.

---

## 🕒 HOUR 2: The Mentorship Breakout (1:00 to 2:00)

**1:00 - 1:15 | Break & Grouping**
*   Take a quick 15-minute break. 
*   **Coordinator Action:** Assign your 13 "Startup & Innovation Coordinators" to group the pitching students with the Teachers based on their domain (e.g., Tech ideas go to CS teachers, Hardware ideas go to Mechanical teachers).

**1:15 - 2:00 | The "Fix-It" Session (Mentorship)**
*   The students sit with the teachers and your coordinators.
*   **The Goal:** The teachers and coordinators use the **"3-Sentence Rule"** (Problem, Solution, Impact) to fix the students' messy pitches. They help the students make their ideas sound professional and realistic. 
*   *(While this happens, you should be walking around the room, taking pictures, and talking to the teachers to make sure they are engaged).*

---

## 🕒 HOUR 3: The Final Pitch & The Portal (2:00 to 3:00)

**2:00 - 2:45 | "Refined Pitches" (Round 2)**
*   The students come back to the stage and pitch their ideas again, but this time using the professional 3-sentence structure the teachers taught them.
*   The difference will be amazing, and the teachers will feel proud that they helped.

**2:45 - 2:50 | The Official Photos (Crucial for Points)**
*   **Social Media Coordinators Action:** Get EVERYONE (Teachers, 36 Coordinators, Students) into a massive group photo. Make sure the IIC banner or a whiteboard saying "IIC Idea Cafe" is in the background. You need these photos for the portal!

**2:50 - 3:00 | Convener's Closing & Next Steps**
*   You take the stage one last time. 
*   Thank the teachers specifically for their time.
*   Tell the students: *"The next step is the Prototype Bootcamp. Take what you learned today and start figuring out how to build a basic version of your idea."*
*   Dismiss the room!
