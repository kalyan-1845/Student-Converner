# Vice Principal's Speech: Idea Cafe

*(Optional script for the Vice Principal to read at the opening of the event)*

1. "Good morning to all the students, faculty members, and the IIC team gathered here today."
2. "Today is a very proud moment for ACE Engineering College because we are stepping beyond just traditional academics."
3. "Through the Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC), mandated by the Ministry of Education, we are building a true startup culture on our campus."
4. "The goal of an engineering college is not just to produce students who look for jobs, but to create innovators who provide jobs."
5. "This 'Idea Cafe' is your showroom. It is the bridge between the technology you build in the labs and the commercial market."
6. "I want you all to pitch without fear today—nobody has a perfect idea on day one, and our faculty mentors are here to guide you."
7. "The best ideas from today will not just stay in this room; they will be pushed to the national level on the Government's YUKTI portal."
8. "I want to thank the faculty members for taking the time to mentor these young minds."
9. "I also want to appreciate our Student Convener, Kalyan Reddy, and the entire 36-member coordinator team for organizing this massive initiative."
10. "Let’s start the pitches, and I look forward to seeing the next big startup come from ACE Engineering College!"
