# Execution Guide: The Lean Startup Bootcamp (Event 2)

If the Idea Cafe was about **talking**, this Bootcamp is about **building**. 
Your goal is to teach the students how to build a Minimum Viable Product (MVP). 

**What is an MVP?** 
It is the cheapest, fastest version of an idea. If a student wants to build a $50,000 food delivery app, their MVP is just a free WhatsApp group where people can text them food orders. You have to teach them to test ideas for *free*.

Here is exactly how you execute this 2-hour event without needing an expensive guest speaker:

---

## 🕒 HOUR 1: The Masterclass (0:00 - 1:00)

**0:00 - 0:10 | Convener's Welcome**
*   Welcome the students back. Tell them: *"At the Idea Cafe, you gave us the vision. Today, we figure out how to build it in 48 hours with zero money."*

**0:10 - 0:40 | The Y-Combinator Video Screening**
*   You do not need a guest speaker. Put the projector on and play a famous startup video.
*   **Search YouTube for:** *"Y Combinator How to Plan an MVP"* (Michael Seibel). It is a legendary 15-minute video.
*   Have the students take notes. This is world-class Silicon Valley knowledge playing right in your college.

**0:40 - 1:00 | Faculty Discussion**
*   Have a business or tech faculty member stand up and discuss the video. Ask the students: *"How can we apply this Silicon Valley mindset to the ideas you pitched at the Idea Cafe?"*

---

## 🕒 HOUR 2: The "Build It" Workshop (1:00 - 2:00)

**1:00 - 1:45 | The MVP Worksheet**
*   Give every student a blank piece of paper. Tell them they have 45 minutes to write down the answers to these 3 questions:
    1. **What is the ONE core feature of your idea?** (Cut out all the extra stuff).
    2. **Who is your very first customer?** (Be specific. Not "everyone", but "2nd-year CSE students").
    3. **How can you test this manually by this Friday for 0 Rupees?**
*   **Coordinator Action:** Your 13 Startup Coordinators must walk around the room, look at the papers, and force the students to keep their answers simple. If a student says "I need to build an AI app", the coordinator must say "No, how do you test it without an app?"

**1:45 - 2:00 | The Wrap Up & Photos**
*   Have 2 or 3 students stand up and read their MVP plan to the room.
*   **Social Media Coordinators:** Take the 5 official photos. We need pictures of the YouTube video playing on the screen, and pictures of the students writing on their worksheets!

**Next Step:** Tell the students to actually go test their MVP over the weekend.
