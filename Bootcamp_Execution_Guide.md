# Execution Guide: Lean Startup Bootcamp (Coordinator Led)

**Duration:** 3 Hours
**Venue:** Room 2408 (Saturday 01-08-2026, 9:30 AM)
**Objective:** The Convener (Kalyan) takes a step back. The **13 Startup & Innovation Coordinators** take the lead to evaluate prototypes, classify business models, and grill the students on their business potential for SIH (Smart India Hackathon).

---

## 🕒 HOUR 1: Prototype Review & Classification (0:00 - 1:00)

**0:00 - 0:15 | Convener's Quick Welcome**
*   Kalyan takes the stage for just 10 minutes.
*   "Welcome back. Today is not about talking, it is about building. I am handing this session over to your IIC Startup Coordinators. They are going to evaluate what you brought today."

**0:15 - 1:00 | The Coordinator Classification (Coordinators take over)**
*   The 13 Coordinators walk around the room and talk to every single team. 
*   **The Task:** The Coordinators must look at what the student brought (a sketch, a basic website, a physical model) and help them CLASSIFY their startup. 
*   *Coordinator Script:* "Is your idea a **SaaS** (Software as a Service - like Zoom), a **Service** (like a consulting agency), an **E-Commerce/Marketplace** (like Amazon), or **Hardware**? You must know what category you are in."

---

## 🕒 HOUR 2: The 5-Question Grill (1:00 - 2:00)

**1:00 - 2:00 | Business Potential Evaluation**
*   The 13 Coordinators sit down with the teams. Based on the WhatsApp message sent earlier, the coordinators must aggressively (but politely) grill the students on the 5 core questions:
    1. What exact problem are you solving?
    2. Who exactly faces this problem? (Define the customer).
    3. What is your solution?
    4. Why is your solution different from competitors?
    5. Can this scale into a sustainable business?
*   **The Rule:** If a student cannot answer these 5 questions clearly, the coordinator must force them to rewrite their answers on a piece of paper until it makes sense.

---

## 🕒 HOUR 3: SIH Prep & Official Photos (2:00 - 3:00)

**2:00 - 2:45 | Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Alignment**
*   Select the top 3 or 4 teams who had the best answers during the "Grill" session. 
*   Have them stand up and present their 5 answers to the whole room.
*   Coordinators explain how these 5 questions are exactly what the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) judges look for, so they are already one step ahead.

**2:45 - 3:00 | The MoE Portal Proof**
*   **Social Media Team:** Take the 5 official photos. 
    *   *Photo 1:* Coordinators talking to students at their desks.
    *   *Photo 2:* A student showing their basic prototype/laptop to a coordinator.
    *   *Photo 3:* The final massive group photo of everyone in Room 2408.
*   Kalyan does a 2-minute closing, thanking the coordinators for leading the session.
