# IIC Coordinator Playbook: The Registration Phone Call

**ATTENTION ALL 22 IIC COORDINATORS:**
When you are calling the students who have registered for the Idea Cafe, you must sound like an official representative of the college. If you explain things poorly on the phone, the students will not show up. 

Use this exact script when you call them. Do not use informal language or sound unsure.

---

## 📞 The Official Phone Call Script

**1. The Introduction**
"Hello, am I speaking to [Student's Name]? 
Hi, I am calling from the **Institution's Innovation Council (IIC)** at ACE Engineering College. I am calling to officially confirm your registration for the upcoming Idea Cafe."

**2. The Confirmation**
"We see that you registered [Solo / as a Group]. I just want to confirm that you are ready to present your idea on [Insert Date] at [Insert Venue/Time]. Are you and your team confirmed to attend?"

*(Wait for their answer)*

**3. The Pitching Instructions (Setting them at ease)**
"Great. As a reminder, you do NOT need a PPT or a formal business presentation. This is an open Cafe format. We just want you to come and explain your raw idea to our mentors. You can bring a prototype if you have one, but speaking is perfectly fine."

**4. The Value (Why they should care)**
"Please make sure you are prepared, because the best ideas presented at this Cafe will be selected for mentorship by IIT Alumni (PALS) and will be officially uploaded to the Ministry of Education's YUKTI Portal for potential funding. We take this very seriously."

**5. The Sign-off**
"Do you have any questions? 
*(Answer any questions using the FAQ below)* 
Great, we look forward to seeing you at the Idea Cafe. Thank you."

---

## ❓ FAQ: Answering Student Doubts Over the Phone

**Doubt 1: "Is this event actually real/serious?"**
**Your Answer:** "Yes, absolutely. The IIC is a direct initiative by the Ministry of Education. This isn't just a college activity; the ideas we select are put into a national government pipeline (YUKTI). That is why we are calling to confirm your spot."

**Doubt 2: "I'm scared my idea isn't good enough."**
**Your Answer:** "That is exactly what the Idea Cafe is for. Nobody has a perfect idea on day one. Our mentors are here to listen to your basic concept and help you make it better. Just show up and pitch."

**Doubt 3: "When exactly is it happening?"**
**Your Answer:** "We will be dropping the final, exact timings in the official WhatsApp group shortly. Make sure you are active in that group."
