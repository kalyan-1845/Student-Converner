# IIC Event 2: Lean Startup & MVP Bootcamp

**MoE Portal Category:** Session on Lean Start-up & Minimum Viable Product (Quarter IV)
**Responsible Team:** Startup & Innovation Coordinators (13 Members)

Since the Idea Cafe is done, you now have a group of students who have raw ideas. The biggest mistake students make is thinking they need to spend 6 months writing code or spending money to build their idea. 

This Bootcamp will teach them the concept of a **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**—how to test their idea in 24 hours with zero money.

---

## 📅 Step 1: The Logistics
*   **Duration:** 2 Hours
*   **Speaker:** You need a faculty member (or an industry guest if possible) who understands basic business, OR you can play a high-quality 20-minute YouTube video from Y Combinator about "How to build an MVP", followed by a discussion.
*   **Target Audience:** Invite everyone who pitched at the Idea Cafe, plus anyone else who wants to learn about startups. You need at least 40 students to meet the MoE requirement.
