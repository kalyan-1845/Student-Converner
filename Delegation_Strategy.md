# IIC Convener Delegation Strategy

**Team Size:** 36 Coordinators
**Convener:** B. Kalyan Reddy

As the Convener, you have an incredible structure here. You have 36 coordinators split perfectly into the 5 official MoE IIC categories. 
To execute the **12 events in our 6-Week Mega Sprint**, you must assign specific events to specific teams. Do not let all 36 people try to do everything at once!

Here is your exact delegation strategy:

---

## 📱 1. Social Media Coordinators (8 Members)
**Your Heaviest Lifters:** G. Prem, M. Surya Teja, Vaishnavi, Goutam, Aditya, Rohan, Rishi, Akhil.
*   **Their Job:** They are required at **EVERY** event. 
*   **Tasks:** Taking the 8+ photos per event, recording 2-minute video clips, designing posters in Canva, managing the WhatsApp groups, and preparing the PDF reports for the MoE portal upload. 

## 🚀 2. Startup & Innovation Coordinators (13 Members Combined)
**The Core Builders:** Satvendra, Aiswarya, Raviraj, Dhruvika, etc.
*   **Their Job:** Managing the actual startups and student ideas.
*   **Events Assigned to them:** 
    1. The Idea Cafe (Calling registered students, managing the room).
    2. Prototype Development Bootcamp (Mentoring the ideas).
    3. The Ultimate B-Plan Pitch Competition (The Grand Finale).
    4. YUKTI Portal Submissions.

## ⚖️ 3. IPR Coordinators (7 Members)
**The Protectors:** Ravi Babu, Dakshitha, Tasya, Varshini, Pranitha, Harshitha, Rimsha.
*   **Their Job:** Handling the legal and patent side of innovation.
*   **Events Assigned to them:** 
    1. Organizing the **IPR & Patent Awareness Workshop** in August. They need to coordinate with the faculty member presenting the PPT and ensure a big crowd attends.
    2. "Out of the Box" Design Thinking Workshop.

## 🤝 4. Internship Coordinators (8 Members)
**The Networkers:** G. Vijay, Sathvika Rao, Adithya Varma, Srikar, Bala Sindhu, Saipardhiv, Sri Ram Reddy, Amulya.
*   **Their Job:** Connecting the college to the outside world (Industry and Incubators).
*   **Events Assigned to them:** 
    1. Finding the guest speaker for the **"My Story" Founder Session**.
    2. Organizing the bus and permissions for the **Field Visit to an Incubation Center**.
    3. Reaching out to PALS mentors.

---

### 👑 The Convener's Role
You do not do any of the tasks above. Your job is to check in with the 5 Team Leads (pick one leader from each of the 5 categories above) every 3 days and ask: *"Is your event ready? Do you need me to talk to the Vice Principal to get a permission slip signed?"*
