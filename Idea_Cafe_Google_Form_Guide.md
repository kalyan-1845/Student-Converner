# Google Form Structure: IIC X ACE Idea Cafe Registration

*Use this guide to quickly build your Google Form. Just copy and paste the text below into the matching fields in Google Forms!*

---

## 📄 FORM HEADER (Title & Description)

**Form Title:** 
IIC x ACE: Idea Cafe Registration 💡

**Form Description:**
Got an idea? We want to hear it! The IIC x ACE Idea Cafe is open to everyone. There are absolutely no restrictions on the domain—if you have an idea, we accept it! You can participate solo or as a group (up to 5 members).

**IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS:**
1) Present your idea in your own way.
2) A PPT is NOT mandatory.
3) Use any form of presentation you are comfortable with (Video, PPT, Prototype, or just speaking).

---

## 📑 SECTION 1: Participant / Team Leader Details

*Add these questions to the first page of your form.*

**Question 1: Are you registering as a Solo participant or a Group?**
* **Type:** Multiple Choice (Turn on "Go to section based on answer" by clicking the 3 dots in the bottom right corner of the question block)
* **Option 1:** Solo Participant  👉 *(Set to: Go to Section 3)*
* **Option 2:** Group (2 to 5 members) 👉 *(Set to: Go to Section 2)*

**Question 2: Name (Team Leader if Group)**
* **Type:** Short Answer

**Question 3: Phone Number**
* **Type:** Short Answer

**Question 4: College Email ID**
* **Type:** Short Answer

**Question 5: Year of Study**
* **Type:** Multiple Choice 
* **Options:** 1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year, 4th Year

**Question 6: Branch**
* **Type:** Short Answer (or Multiple Choice with college branches like CSE, ECE, IT, etc.)

**Question 7: Section**
* **Type:** Short Answer

**Question 8: Do you already have a working Prototype or Model? (Optional)**
* **Type:** Multiple Choice
* **Options:** Yes / No
*(Note: Do not make this mandatory. If they leave it blank, you can just call them later to ask!)*

**Question 9: What is the Name or brief idea of your project? (Optional)**
* **Type:** Short Answer 
*(Note: Not mandatory, just to give you some clarity before they present.)*

---

## 👥 SECTION 2: Group Details
*Create a new section in Google Forms and title it "Group Details". Only people who select "Group" will see this page.*

**Section Description:**
Since you are registering as a group, please provide the details of your teammates below (Max 5 members including you).

**Question 10: Team Name (Optional)**
* **Type:** Short Answer

**Question 11: Teammate 2 Details (Name, Phone No, Email, Year, Branch, Section)**
* **Type:** Paragraph (Long Answer)

**Question 12: Teammate 3 Details (Leave blank if not applicable)**
* **Type:** Paragraph (Long Answer)

**Question 13: Teammate 4 Details (Leave blank if not applicable)**
* **Type:** Paragraph (Long Answer)

**Question 14: Teammate 5 Details (Leave blank if not applicable)**
* **Type:** Paragraph (Long Answer)

## ⚙️ IMPORTANT: How to add the WhatsApp Link AFTER they submit
*To make sure they only get the WhatsApp link AFTER they click submit, you have to change a setting in Google Forms.*

1. Go to the top of your Google Form and click on the **"Settings"** tab.
2. Scroll down to **"Presentation"** and click the drop-down arrow.
3. Look for **"Confirmation message"** and click **"Edit"**.
4. Paste this exact message and click Save:

> **Thank you for registering for the Idea Cafe! 💡**
> **You MUST join our official WhatsApp group for all event updates, timings, and announcements. Click the link below to join now:**
> **[Paste Your WhatsApp Group Link Here]**
