# Coordinator Target List: Idea Specific Questions

**Attention Coordinators:** We have looked at the actual registration data. Many of these ideas have massive gaps or are too complex. When you sit at the tables with these specific teams on Saturday, you must ask them these exact questions to force them to think like true founders.

---

### 🎯 Target 1: Tasya (Idea: SentiPulse Hub - Wearable Laser Mapping/SOS)
*   **The Problem:** This sounds incredibly expensive and complicated to build (hardware + lasers).
*   **Your Grill Question:** *"Building hardware rings with laser mapping costs lakhs of rupees. How can you test the core feature (the Fall SOS or currency scanning) using just a standard smartphone app by next week, without building any physical hardware?"*

### 🎯 Target 2: Chavan Srikar / AIML DASHERS (Idea: Class Attendance SMS System)
*   **The Problem:** Almost every college already has an ERP system (like TCS iON or BeES) that sends automated SMS to parents.
*   **Your Grill Question:** *"Most colleges already pay lakhs for big ERP systems that do attendance and SMS. Why would our Principal buy your separate system instead of just using the one we already have? What makes yours better/cheaper?"*

### 🎯 Target 3: Barla Sujan Kumar / Team Sristi (Idea: AI Security Testing)
*   **The Problem:** Cybersecurity is dominated by massive billion-dollar companies (like Crowdstrike). 
*   **Your Grill Question:** *"Who is your actual customer? A normal college student won't pay for this, and a big bank will only trust a massive company. Who is the exact person that will pay you for this AI security testing?"*

### 🎯 Target 4: Aishwarya / Synapse Squad (Idea: Urban Cool AI)
*   **The Problem:** The name sounds cool, but nobody knows what it actually does.
*   **Your Grill Question:** *"I don't understand what this is. Explain 'Urban Cool AI' to me in one sentence without using the word AI. Are you selling a software app, a physical AC unit, or a consulting service?"*

### 🎯 Target 5: Sannidhanam Vaishnavi / Fusionx (Idea: Earphones Holder)
*   **The Problem:** The market is shifting completely to wireless earbuds (Airpods, boAt Airdopes). 
*   **Your Grill Question:** *"90% of people are switching to wireless Bluetooth earbuds. Is the market for wired earphone holders dying? Who is your target customer for this today?"*

### 🎯 Target 6: Regu Pooja (Idea: SafeDocAI app)
*   **The Problem:** Medical data has massive legal and privacy restrictions.
*   **Your Grill Question:** *"Is this app for doctors or patients? If an AI makes a mistake with a medical document, who is legally responsible? How are you handling data privacy laws?"*

### 🎯 Target 7: Victor Isaac Chintha (Idea: Musicman)
*   **The Problem:** The name means nothing. It could be an app, a speaker, or a service.
*   **Your Grill Question:** *"Musicman is just a name. What are you actually building? Are you trying to compete with Spotify, are you building a physical instrument, or is it an AI that generates music? You must define exactly what the product is."*

### 🎯 Target 8: Aryan.M (Idea: Rn-Ai sales engineer)
*   **The Problem:** The B2B sales software market is already crowded with massive AI bots.
*   **Your Grill Question:** *"Companies already spend millions on Salesforce and existing AI sales bots. Why would a real business trust your AI to talk to their important clients? What makes your sales AI better than what they already use?"*

---
**Convener's Note:** For the students who did not list an idea on the registration form, your first job is to force them to write down 3 problems they face in their daily college life, and pick one to solve!
