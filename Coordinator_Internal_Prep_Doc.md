# The Ultimate 2-Hour Coordinator Training Script

**Convener Instructions:** This document contains the **Live Roleplay Gauntlet**. You will sit on one side of the room, and the 40 coordinators on the other. You will pitch these 8 dummy ideas. For each idea, they must rapid-fire 15 questions at you. 

*Below is exactly what you should answer for every single question. If they ask a weak question, reject it. Make them read these questions to understand how deep a real business interrogation goes.*

---

## 🎭 Scenario 1: E-Commerce (The College Food Delivery App)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building an app like Swiggy, but only for our college canteen."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Who is going to do the physical delivery? *(A: 1st-year students).*
2. What is the delivery fee? *(A: 10 Rupees).*
3. How do students pay? Cash or UPI? *(A: UPI only).*
4. How does the canteen receive the order? *(A: A tablet in the kitchen).*
5. Are you delivering to classrooms or just hostels? *(A: Hostels).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. If the food is 50 Rupees, and you charge 10 Rupees, how do you make enough profit to pay the delivery personnel? *(A: I will rely on massive volume, doing 500 deliveries a day).*
7. What happens if the delivery personnel steal or eat the food? *(A: I will ban them from the app).*
8. If a student cancels the order while the food is in transit, who pays the canteen? *(A: The student's account gets suspended until they pay the fine).*
9. How do you prevent the food from getting cold during delivery? *(A: We will use insulated bags).*
10. What if 100 people order at exactly 1:00 PM? How does your system handle the server crash? *(A: We will use AWS auto-scaling servers).*

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **The MVP:** Building an app takes three months. Why can't we test this today by simply making a WhatsApp group? *(A: Because an app looks more professional to investors).*
12. **Liability:** If a student contracts food poisoning, who is legally responsible? You or the canteen? *(A: The canteen, we are just the delivery partner).*
13. **Competition:** What happens when real food delivery apps (like Swiggy) secure permission to deliver inside the campus? *(A: We will have better local relationships and cheaper fees).*
14. **Licensing:** Do you require an FSSAI food license to operate a delivery business? *(A: I am not sure, I need to check the laws).*
15. **Scale:** How does this business scale beyond our single college campus? *(A: We will launch the exact same app in 10 other colleges next year).*

## 🎭 Scenario 2: Hardware / IoT (The Smart AC Helmet)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building a motorcycle helmet with a built-in Air Conditioner."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Where is the battery located? *(A: Inside the back of the helmet).*
2. Is the helmet waterproof? *(A: Yes).*
3. How long does the battery last on a single charge? *(A: 2 hours).*
4. Does the AC mechanism generate excessive noise? *(A: A little bit).*
5. How do you charge it? *(A: USB-C).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. An AC compressor is inherently heavy. How heavy will this helmet be on the rider's neck? *(A: Around 3 Kilograms).*
7. How much will it cost the end consumer to purchase? *(A: 15,000 Rupees).*
8. Air conditioners exhaust hot air. Where does the thermal exhaust go? *(A: Out through vents in the back).*
9. What happens if the lithium battery overheats and explodes in close proximity to the rider's head? *(A: We will use heat-resistant casing).*
10. Does the cold air cause the visor to fog up, obstructing the driver's vision? *(A: We will add an anti-fog coating).*

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **The MVP:** Hardware prototyping costs lakhs of rupees. How do you test if bikers actually want this using just a free survey today? *(A: I will run Facebook ads showing a 3D model to see if people click 'Buy').*
12. **Certification:** Helmets require strict ISI/BIS safety certifications in India. How will you pass crash tests with a hollowed-out helmet? *(A: We will use carbon fiber to make it stronger).*
13. **Physics:** Is a miniature battery-powered fan genuinely strong enough to combat 45-degree Indian summer heat? *(A: It will at least cool their face down a few degrees).*
14. **Liability:** If a biker crashes and the AC compressor causes severe injury, who gets sued? *(A: I will put a legal waiver in the instruction manual).*
15. **Manufacturing:** How do you manufacture 1,000 units if you do not own a manufacturing facility? *(A: I will outsource it to a factory in China).*

## 🎭 Scenario 3: Generative AI (The AI Resume Writer)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building an AI website that automatically writes a perfect engineering resume."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Who is your exact target audience? *(A: Final year engineering students).*
2. Is the service free or paid? *(A: 100 Rupees per resume).*
3. Does it export as a PDF or Word Document? *(A: PDF).*
4. Is the website mobile-friendly? *(A: Yes).*
5. How does the AI know my skills? *(A: You input them into a text box).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. ChatGPT already writes resumes perfectly for free. Why would anyone pay you? *(A: Mine is specifically trained on engineering keywords).*
7. Are the generated resumes ATS (Applicant Tracking System) friendly? *(A: Yes, they use standard ATS templates).*
8. If 1,000 students use your application, won't they all end up with identical resume templates? *(A: We have 50 different templates they can choose from).*
9. Where is the users' private data securely stored? *(A: On Firebase servers).*
10. Does the platform support multiple languages? *(A: Only English for now).*

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Hallucinations:** If your AI 'hallucinates' and inserts fake skills on a student's resume, and they subsequently get fired, are you legally liable? *(A: The user has to manually review it before downloading).*
12. **The Pivot:** Instead of selling directly to students for 100 Rupees, could you sell this as a B2B SaaS platform to the College Placement Cell for 1 Lakh? *(A: That is a great idea, I should try that).*
13. **API Costs:** OpenAI charges you per generation. How will you afford the API costs if 10,000 people use it? *(A: The 100 Rupee fee covers the API cost with a 90% profit margin).*
14. **The MVP:** Can you test this manually today by taking 5 students' data and writing the prompts yourself? *(A: Yes, I can do that on WhatsApp tonight).*
15. **Defensibility:** What prevents a 1st-year computer science student from cloning your exact website in two days? *(A: Nothing, but I will market it better than them).*

## 🎭 Scenario 4: MedTech (The AI Skin Disease Scanner)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building an app where you take a photo of your skin, and the AI diagnoses if you have cancer."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Is this application intended for doctors or the general public? *(A: The general public).*
2. Do you need an active internet connection to process the image? *(A: Yes).*
3. Does the AI work accurately across all skin tones? *(A: We hope so).*
4. What is the pricing model? *(A: Free, supported by ads).*
5. How long does the diagnostic scan take? *(A: 5 seconds).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. Where are you sourcing the thousands of verified medical photographs needed to train this AI accurately? *(A: From open-source medical datasets online).*
7. Smartphone cameras vary significantly in quality. How does a blurry photo affect the AI's diagnosis? *(A: The app will reject the photo and ask them to retake it).*
8. Does a licensed human doctor verify the AI's results before the patient is notified? *(A: No, the AI does it instantly).*
9. What is your statistical false-positive rate? *(A: Around 5%, but we are improving it).*
10. Where is this highly sensitive medical data being stored? *(A: We don't store it; we delete the photo after scanning).*

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Legality:** Medical diagnosis is highly illegal without a medical license. What if your AI tells a patient they are healthy, but they actually have cancer? *(A: We will put a disclaimer saying 'This is not medical advice').*
12. **Regulations:** Have you applied for CDSCO (Indian FDA) approval for medical software devices? *(A: No, I didn't know I needed that).*
13. **The Pivot:** Instead of diagnosing cancer directly, could the app simply connect the user to a real, licensed dermatologist? *(A: Yes, we can make it a doctor-booking app instead).*
14. **Malpractice:** Who is paying the exorbitant medical malpractice insurance premiums? *(A: We don't have insurance yet).*
15. **The MVP:** How do you test if people actually want this application today without writing a single line of AI code? *(A: I can ask people to WhatsApp me photos and I will show them to a real doctor).*

## 🎭 Scenario 5: EdTech (The Blockchain Degree Generator)

**You Pitch:** *"I want to put all college degrees on the Blockchain so they cannot be forged."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. What exact problem does this solve for the institution? *(A: It prevents fake certificates).*
2. Who pays for the service? The student or the college? *(A: The college).*
3. Which specific blockchain network are you utilizing? *(A: Ethereum).*
4. Is the ledger public or private? *(A: Public).*
5. Who is responsible for entering the grades into the blockchain? *(A: The faculty).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. Why utilize expensive Blockchain technology instead of issuing a highly secure, digitally signed PDF? *(A: Because Blockchain is impossible to hack).*
7. What happens if a student loses their crypto wallet private keys? Is their degree inaccessible forever? *(A: We will keep a backup database).*
8. Who pays the transaction "Gas Fees" every time a new certificate is minted? *(A: The college pays it as an operational cost).*
9. What happens if a teacher enters the wrong grade? Blockchains are immutable and cannot be edited. *(A: We will have to issue a new, corrected block and invalidate the old one).*
10. Are there vulnerabilities in your smart contracts that could allow hackers to issue fraudulent degrees? *(A: We will hire auditors to check the code).*

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Adoption:** Colleges already utilize massive ERP systems. How do you intend to force the Principal to abandon their current system and adopt yours? *(A: We will offer to integrate our blockchain directly into their existing ERP as a plugin).*
12. **Regulation:** Do the UGC and AICTE legally recognize blockchain-issued degrees in India? *(A: They are starting to explore it, we want to be first).*
13. **The MVP:** Can you test this concept manually today with just 10 students using a standard relational database? *(A: Yes, but the blockchain buzzword helps us get funding).*
14. **Data Laws:** Under Indian data privacy laws, a user possesses the "Right to be Forgotten." How do you delete data from an un-deletable blockchain? *(A: We only store a 'hash' on the blockchain, not their personal name).*
15. **Sales:** Who is the very first Principal you are going to pitch this product to? *(A: The Principal of ACE Engineering College).*

## 🎭 Scenario 6: FinTech (The Student Micro-Loan App)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building an app that issues 500 Rupee micro-loans to students who run out of pocket money."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Where is the capital coming from to issue these loans? *(A: My personal savings).*
2. What is the maximum allowable loan amount? *(A: 1,000 Rupees).*
3. What is the interest rate? *(A: 10% per month).*
4. How quickly is the money transferred to the borrower? *(A: Instantly via UPI).*
5. What is the repayment window? *(A: 30 days).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. College students lack formal income. How do you physically compel them to repay you if they delete the application? *(A: I will require them to give me their parents' phone numbers).*
7. What is your projected default rate? (What percentage of students will never repay the loan?) *(A: I am hoping less than 5%).*
8. How do you conduct formal KYC (Know Your Customer) verifications on a student? *(A: They must upload their Aadhar and College ID).*
9. Do you run checks on their CIBIL credit score? *(A: No, students usually don't have credit scores).*
10. What protocol is in place if the student changes their phone number? *(A: We track their device ID and college roll number).*

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Licensing:** Lending money formally requires strict RBI (Reserve Bank of India) NBFC licenses. How are you operating this legally? *(A: We will partner with an existing bank to use their license).*
12. **Usury:** Charging 10% per month is considered usury (loan sharking) in many jurisdictions. How do you legally justify this interest rate? *(A: We will call it a 'Processing Fee' instead of interest).*
13. **Harassment:** If you contact their parents to recover the funds, can you be sued for harassment or extortion? *(A: We will put it in the Terms and Conditions that they agree to it).*
14. **The MVP:** Can you test this assumption today by lending 100 Rupees to 5 friends and tracking if they repay you? *(A: Yes, I can do that on Google Pay right now).*
15. **Disaster:** What is your contingency plan if 100 students borrow money on Day 1, and zero students repay you on Day 30? *(A: My business goes bankrupt).*

## 🎭 Scenario 7: GreenTech (The Smart Dustbin)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building a smart dustbin that automatically sorts dry and wet waste."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. What technology makes this dustbin 'smart'? *(A: Sensors and AI cameras).*
2. Where does it draw power from? *(A: It plugs directly into a wall outlet).*
3. Is it designed for indoor or outdoor usage? *(A: Indoors).*
4. What are its physical dimensions? *(A: Standard kitchen size).*
5. Who is the primary target buyer? *(A: Smart cities and municipalities).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. How often does the camera lens become obscured by wet garbage, rendering the AI useless? *(A: It has a self-cleaning wiper).*
7. What is the mechanical lifespan of the robotic sorting arm? *(A: About 5 years before it needs maintenance).*
8. How do you protect the unit from theft or vandalism if placed on the street? *(A: It will be bolted into the concrete).*
9. What is the verified accuracy percentage of the sorting algorithm? *(A: Currently 85% accurate).*
10. Does the unit emit foul odors because the mechanical sorting process takes time? *(A: It has an ozone generator to kill smells).*

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Cost ROI:** A smart dustbin costs 50,000 Rupees. A standard plastic one costs 500 Rupees. How do you mathematically prove to the government that they will save money? *(A: They will save money by not having to hire manual labor to sort the trash later).*
12. **Procurement:** Government tenders are notoriously difficult to secure. How will a college student win a municipal contract? *(A: We will pilot it for free in a private mall first to build a case study).*
13. **The MVP:** How do you validate that citizens care about automated waste sorting today without actually building a robot? *(A: I will build a fake 'smart' bin, hide inside it, and sort the trash by hand to see if people use it).*
14. **E-Waste:** Aren't you contributing to electronic waste by putting silicon chips and batteries inside dustbins? *(A: Our electronics are fully recyclable).*
15. **Patents:** Have you secured the hardware patents for the proprietary robotic sorting arm? *(A: We are filing a provisional patent next week).*

## 🎭 Scenario 8: Services / Marketplace (The College Fiverr)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building a freelance marketplace exclusively for our college, where students can hire peers to complete assignments."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. What is your revenue model? *(A: We take a 10% commission).*
2. What categories of jobs are permitted? *(A: Coding, homework, graphic design).*
3. Do you hold the funds in a secure escrow account? *(A: Yes).*
4. How do users verify they are legitimate students? *(A: College ID card upload).*
5. Is this platform a website or a mobile application? *(A: A website).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. How do you prevent students from connecting on your platform, but processing the payment in cash in the canteen to circumvent your 10% fee? *(A: We will ban accounts that share phone numbers in the chat).*
7. What is the dispute resolution process if a buyer refuses to pay, claiming the submitted work is substandard? *(A: We have a manual arbitration team of seniors).*
8. How do you mitigate the creation of fake 5-star reviews? *(A: You can only leave a review if you actually paid through the escrow).*
9. Is there a minimum financial threshold required for a freelancer to withdraw their earnings? *(A: Yes, 500 Rupees).*
10. How do you vet and guarantee the quality of the freelancers on the platform? *(A: The review system naturally filters out bad freelancers).*

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **The Chicken and Egg:** A marketplace requires both buyers and sellers simultaneously. How do you acquire buyers when there are no freelancers, and vice versa? *(A: I will manually act as a freelancer for the first 50 jobs to guarantee supply).*
12. **Liability:** If a student hires someone to complete their homework, and the professor detects plagiarism, can the college administration suspend you for facilitating academic dishonesty? *(A: We will add a rule that bans 'academic cheating' and only allows 'tutoring').*
13. **Margins:** Payment gateways (like Razorpay) charge a 2% processing fee. If you only take 10%, your profit margin is critically thin. How does the business survive? *(A: We will eventually raise our fee to 20%).*
14. **The MVP:** Can you validate this marketplace today by acting as a manual broker in a WhatsApp group? *(A: Yes, I can start the WhatsApp group tonight).*
15. **Scale:** How do you successfully expand this highly localized platform to a second college where you have zero network connections? *(A: We will hire a 'Campus Ambassador' at the second college).*
