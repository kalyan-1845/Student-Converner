# The Ultimate 2-Hour Coordinator Training Script

**Convener Instructions:** This document contains the **Live Roleplay Gauntlet**. You will sit on one side of the room, and the 40 coordinators on the other. You will pitch these 8 dummy ideas. For each idea, they must rapid-fire 15 questions at you. 

*If they ask a weak question, reject it. Make them read these questions to understand how deep a real business interrogation goes.*

---

### 🎭 Scenario 1: E-Commerce (The College Food Delivery App)
*   **You Pitch:** *"I am building an app like Swiggy, but only for our college canteen."*
*   **Simple Questions (Level 1):**
    1. Who is going to do the physical delivery? *(A: 1st-year students).*
    2. What is the delivery fee? *(A: 10 Rupees).*
    3. How do students pay? Cash or UPI? *(A: UPI only).*
    4. How does the canteen receive the order? *(A: A tablet in the kitchen).*
    5. Are you delivering to classrooms or just hostels? *(A: Hostels).*
*   **Medium Questions (Level 2):**
    6. If the food is 50 Rs, and you charge 10 Rs, how do you make enough profit to pay the delivery boy?
    7. What happens if the delivery boy steals or eats the food?
    8. If a student cancels the order while the food is on the way, who pays the canteen?
    9. How do you stop the food from getting cold during delivery?
    10. What if 100 people order at exactly 1:00 PM? How does your system handle the crash?
*   **Hard Questions (Level 3):**
    11. **The MVP:** Building an app takes 3 months. Why can't we test this today by just making a WhatsApp group?
    12. **Liability:** If a student gets food poisoning, who is legally responsible? You or the canteen?
    13. **Competition:** What happens when real Swiggy/Zomato starts delivering inside the campus?
    14. **Licensing:** Do you need an FSSAI food license to operate a delivery business?
    15. **Scale:** How does this business grow beyond just our one college campus?

### 🎭 Scenario 2: Hardware / IoT (The Smart AC Helmet)
*   **You Pitch:** *"I am building a motorcycle helmet with a built-in Air Conditioner."*
*   **Simple Questions (Level 1):**
    1. Where does the battery go? *(A: Inside the back of the helmet).*
    2. Is it waterproof? *(A: Yes).*
    3. How long does the battery last? *(A: 2 hours).*
    4. Does the AC make a lot of noise? *(A: A little bit).*
    5. How do you charge it? *(A: USB-C).*
*   **Medium Questions (Level 2):**
    6. An AC compressor is heavy. How heavy is this helmet on someone's neck?
    7. How much will it cost the customer to buy? 
    8. Air conditioners blow hot air out the back. Where does the thermal exhaust go?
    9. What happens if the lithium battery overheats and explodes next to someone's brain?
    10. Does the cold air make the visor fog up so the driver can't see?
*   **Hard Questions (Level 3):**
    11. **The MVP:** Hardware prototyping costs lakhs. How do you test if bikers actually want this using just a free survey today?
    12. **Certification:** Helmets require strict ISI/BIS safety certifications in India. How will you pass crash tests with a hollow helmet?
    13. **Physics:** Is a tiny battery-powered fan actually strong enough to fight 45-degree Indian summer heat?
    14. **Liability:** If a biker crashes and the AC compressor crushes their skull, who gets sued?
    15. **Manufacturing:** How do you manufacture 1,000 of these if you don't own a factory?

### 🎭 Scenario 3: Generative AI (The AI Resume Writer)
*   **You Pitch:** *"I am building an AI website that automatically writes a perfect engineering resume."*
*   **Simple Questions (Level 1):**
    1. Who is your exact target audience? *(A: Final year engineering students).*
    2. Is it free or paid? *(A: 100 Rupees per resume).*
    3. Does it export as a PDF or Word Doc? *(A: PDF).*
    4. Does it work on mobile phones? *(A: Yes).*
    5. How does the AI know my skills? *(A: You type them in a box).*
*   **Medium Questions (Level 2):**
    6. ChatGPT writes resumes perfectly for free. Why would anyone pay you?
    7. Are the resumes ATS (Applicant Tracking System) friendly? 
    8. If 1,000 students use your app, won't they all end up with the exact same resume template?
    9. Where is the users' private data stored? 
    10. Does it support multiple languages?
*   **Hard Questions (Level 3):**
    11. **Hallucinations:** If your AI 'hallucinates' and puts fake skills on a student's resume, and they get fired, are you liable?
    12. **The Pivot:** Instead of selling to students for 100 Rs, can you sell this to the College Placement Cell for 1 Lakh?
    13. **API Costs:** OpenAI charges you every time the AI writes a resume. How will you afford the API costs if 10,000 people use it?
    14. **The MVP:** Can you test this manually today by just taking 5 students' data and writing prompts for them yourself?
    15. **Defensibility:** What stops a 1st-year CSE student from cloning your exact website in 2 days?

### 🎭 Scenario 4: MedTech (The AI Skin Disease Scanner)
*   **You Pitch:** *"I am building an app where you take a photo of your skin, and the AI tells you if you have cancer."*
*   **Simple Questions (Level 1):**
    1. Is this app for doctors or normal people? *(A: Normal people).*
    2. Do you need an internet connection to use it? *(A: Yes).*
    3. Does it work on all skin colors? *(A: We hope so).*
    4. How much does it cost? *(A: Free with ads).*
    5. How long does the scan take? *(A: 5 seconds).*
*   **Medium Questions (Level 2):**
    6. Where are you getting the thousands of medical photos needed to train this AI accurately?
    7. Phone cameras have different qualities. How does a blurry photo affect the AI?
    8. Does a real human doctor verify the AI's results?
    9. What is your false-positive rate?
    10. Where is this highly sensitive medical data stored?
*   **Hard Questions (Level 3):**
    11. **Legality:** Medical diagnosis is highly illegal without a license. What if your AI tells someone they are healthy, but they actually have cancer? 
    12. **Regulations:** Have you applied for CDSCO (Indian FDA) approval for medical software?
    13. **The Pivot:** Instead of diagnosing cancer, can the app just connect the user to a real dermatologist?
    14. **Malpractice:** Who pays the medical malpractice insurance?
    15. **The MVP:** How do you test if people actually want this app today without writing a single line of AI code?

### 🎭 Scenario 5: EdTech (The Blockchain Degree Generator)
*   **You Pitch:** *"I want to put all college degrees on the Blockchain so they can't be faked."*
*   **Simple Questions (Level 1):**
    1. What exact problem does this solve? *(A: Fake certificates).*
    2. Who pays for this? The student or the college? *(A: The college).*
    3. Which blockchain are you using? *(A: Ethereum).*
    4. Is the ledger public or private? *(A: Public).*
    5. Who enters the grades into the blockchain? *(A: The teachers).*
*   **Medium Questions (Level 2):**
    6. Why use expensive Blockchain technology instead of just a secure, digitally signed PDF?
    7. What happens if a student loses their crypto wallet password? Is their degree gone forever?
    8. Who pays the "Gas Fees" every time a new certificate is minted?
    9. What happens if a teacher enters the wrong grade? Blockchains are permanent and cannot be edited.
    10. Are there smart contract bugs that could allow hackers to issue fake degrees?
*   **Hard Questions (Level 3):**
    11. **Adoption:** Colleges already use massive ERP systems. How do you force the Principal to switch to your platform?
    12. **Regulation:** Does UGC/AICTE legally recognize blockchain degrees in India?
    13. **The MVP:** Can you test this manually with just 10 students today using a simple database?
    14. **Data Laws:** Under Indian data laws, a user has the "Right to be Forgotten." How do you delete data from an un-deletable blockchain?
    15. **Sales:** Who is the first Principal you are going to pitch this to?

### 🎭 Scenario 6: FinTech (The Student Micro-Loan App)
*   **You Pitch:** *"I am building an app that gives 500 Rupee micro-loans to students who run out of pocket money."*
*   **Simple Questions (Level 1):**
    1. Where does the money come from? *(A: My own savings).*
    2. What is the maximum loan amount? *(A: 1,000 Rupees).*
    3. What is the interest rate? *(A: 10% per month).*
    4. How fast is the money transferred? *(A: Instantly via UPI).*
    5. How long do they have to pay it back? *(A: 30 days).*
*   **Medium Questions (Level 2):**
    6. College students have no income. How do you force them to pay you back if they delete the app?
    7. What is your expected default rate? (How many will never pay you back?)
    8. How do you do KYC (Know Your Customer) on a student?
    9. Do you check their CIBIL credit score?
    10. What happens if they change their phone number?
*   **Hard Questions (Level 3):**
    11. **Licensing:** Lending money requires strict RBI (Reserve Bank of India) NBFC licenses. How are you doing this legally?
    12. **Usury:** Charging 10% per month is illegal (loan sharking). How do you justify the interest rate?
    13. **Harassment:** If you call their parents to recover the money, can they sue you for harassment?
    14. **The MVP:** Can you test this today by just lending 100 Rupees to 5 friends and seeing if they pay you back?
    15. **Disaster:** What happens if 100 students borrow money on Day 1, and zero students pay you back on Day 30?

### 🎭 Scenario 7: GreenTech (The Smart Dustbin)
*   **You Pitch:** *"I am building a smart dustbin that sorts dry and wet waste automatically."*
*   **Simple Questions (Level 1):**
    1. What makes it smart? *(A: Sensors and AI cameras).*
    2. Where does it get power? *(A: Plugs into the wall).*
    3. Is it for indoors or outdoors? *(A: Indoors).*
    4. How big is it? *(A: Normal kitchen size).*
    5. Who is the target buyer? *(A: Smart cities and municipalities).*
*   **Medium Questions (Level 2):**
    6. How often does the camera lens get covered in wet garbage and stop working?
    7. What is the lifespan of the robotic sorting arm?
    8. How do you protect it from being stolen or vandalized on the street?
    9. What is the actual accuracy rate of the sorting AI?
    10. Does it smell terrible because it takes time to sort the wet waste?
*   **Hard Questions (Level 3):**
    11. **Cost ROI:** A smart dustbin costs 50,000 Rs. A plastic one costs 500 Rs. How do you prove to the government that they will save money?
    12. **Corruption/Procurement:** Government tenders are notoriously hard to win. How will a college student win a municipality contract?
    13. **The MVP:** How do you test if people care about sorted waste today without building a robot?
    14. **E-Waste:** Aren't you just creating more electronic waste by putting chips and batteries inside dustbins?
    15. **Patents:** Do you have the hardware patents for the robotic arm?

### 🎭 Scenario 8: Services / Marketplace (The College Fiverr)
*   **You Pitch:** *"I am building a freelance marketplace just for our college, where students can pay other students to do their assignments."*
*   **Simple Questions (Level 1):**
    1. How do you make money? *(A: I take a 10% commission).*
    2. What kind of jobs are posted? *(A: Coding, homework, graphic design).*
    3. Do you hold the money in escrow? *(A: Yes).*
    4. How do users prove they are real students? *(A: College ID upload).*
    5. Is it a website or an app? *(A: A website).*
*   **Medium Questions (Level 2):**
    6. How do you stop students from just meeting on your platform, and then paying each other in cash to avoid your 10% fee?
    7. What happens if a buyer refuses to pay because the work is "bad"?
    8. How do you prevent fake 5-star reviews?
    9. Is there a minimum payout threshold?
    10. How do you guarantee the quality of the freelancers?
*   **Hard Questions (Level 3):**
    11. **The Chicken and Egg:** A marketplace needs buyers and sellers. How do you get buyers to post jobs when there are no freelancers, and vice versa?
    12. **Liability:** If a student pays someone to do their homework, and the teacher catches them for plagiarism, does the college suspend you?
    13. **Payment Gateways:** Razorpay and Stripe charge 2% fees. If you take 10%, your profit margin is tiny. How do you survive?
    14. **The MVP:** Can you test this today by just acting as a manual broker in a WhatsApp group?
    15. **Scale:** How do you expand this platform to a second college where you don't know anyone?
