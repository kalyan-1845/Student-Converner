# The Ultimate 2-Hour Coordinator Training Script

**Convener Instructions:** This document contains the **Live Roleplay Gauntlet**. You will sit on one side of the room, and the 40 coordinators on the other. You will pitch these 8 dummy ideas. For each idea, they must rapid-fire 15 questions at you. 

*If they ask a weak question, reject it. Make them read these questions to understand how deep a real business interrogation goes.*

---

## 🎭 Scenario 1: E-Commerce (The College Food Delivery App)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building an app like Swiggy, but only for our college canteen."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Who is going to do the physical delivery? *(A: 1st-year students).*
2. What is the delivery fee? *(A: 10 Rupees).*
3. How do students pay? Cash or UPI? *(A: UPI only).*
4. How does the canteen receive the order? *(A: A tablet in the kitchen).*
5. Are you delivering to classrooms or just hostels? *(A: Hostels).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. If the food is 50 Rupees, and you charge 10 Rupees, how do you make enough profit to pay the delivery personnel?
7. What happens if the delivery personnel steal or eat the food?
8. If a student cancels the order while the food is in transit, who pays the canteen?
9. How do you prevent the food from getting cold during delivery?
10. What if 100 people order at exactly 1:00 PM? How does your system handle the server crash?

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **The MVP:** Building an app takes three months. Why can't we test this today by simply making a WhatsApp group?
12. **Liability:** If a student contracts food poisoning, who is legally responsible? You or the canteen?
13. **Competition:** What happens when real food delivery apps (like Swiggy or Zomato) secure permission to deliver inside the campus?
14. **Licensing:** Do you require an FSSAI food license to operate a delivery business?
15. **Scale:** How does this business scale beyond our single college campus?

## 🎭 Scenario 2: Hardware / IoT (The Smart AC Helmet)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building a motorcycle helmet with a built-in Air Conditioner."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Where is the battery located? *(A: Inside the back of the helmet).*
2. Is the helmet waterproof? *(A: Yes).*
3. How long does the battery last on a single charge? *(A: 2 hours).*
4. Does the AC mechanism generate excessive noise? *(A: A little bit).*
5. How do you charge it? *(A: USB-C).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. An AC compressor is inherently heavy. How heavy will this helmet be on the rider's neck?
7. How much will it cost the end consumer to purchase?
8. Air conditioners exhaust hot air. Where does the thermal exhaust go?
9. What happens if the lithium battery overheats and explodes in close proximity to the rider's head?
10. Does the cold air cause the visor to fog up, obstructing the driver's vision?

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **The MVP:** Hardware prototyping costs lakhs of rupees. How do you test if bikers actually want this using just a free survey today?
12. **Certification:** Helmets require strict ISI/BIS safety certifications in India. How will you pass crash tests with a hollowed-out helmet?
13. **Physics:** Is a miniature battery-powered fan genuinely strong enough to combat 45-degree Indian summer heat?
14. **Liability:** If a biker crashes and the AC compressor causes severe injury, who gets sued?
15. **Manufacturing:** How do you manufacture 1,000 units if you do not own a manufacturing facility?

## 🎭 Scenario 3: Generative AI (The AI Resume Writer)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building an AI website that automatically writes a perfect engineering resume."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Who is your exact target audience? *(A: Final year engineering students).*
2. Is the service free or paid? *(A: 100 Rupees per resume).*
3. Does it export as a PDF or Word Document? *(A: PDF).*
4. Is the website mobile-friendly? *(A: Yes).*
5. How does the AI know my skills? *(A: You input them into a text box).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. ChatGPT already writes resumes perfectly for free. Why would anyone pay you?
7. Are the generated resumes ATS (Applicant Tracking System) friendly?
8. If 1,000 students use your application, won't they all end up with identical resume templates?
9. Where is the users' private data securely stored?
10. Does the platform support multiple languages?

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Hallucinations:** If your AI 'hallucinates' and inserts fake skills on a student's resume, and they subsequently get fired, are you legally liable?
12. **The Pivot:** Instead of selling directly to students for 100 Rupees, could you sell this as a B2B SaaS platform to the College Placement Cell for 1 Lakh?
13. **API Costs:** OpenAI charges you per generation. How will you afford the API costs if 10,000 people use it?
14. **The MVP:** Can you test this manually today by taking 5 students' data and writing the prompts yourself?
15. **Defensibility:** What prevents a 1st-year computer science student from cloning your exact website in two days?

## 🎭 Scenario 4: MedTech (The AI Skin Disease Scanner)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building an app where you take a photo of your skin, and the AI diagnoses if you have cancer."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Is this application intended for doctors or the general public? *(A: The general public).*
2. Do you need an active internet connection to process the image? *(A: Yes).*
3. Does the AI work accurately across all skin tones? *(A: We hope so).*
4. What is the pricing model? *(A: Free, supported by ads).*
5. How long does the diagnostic scan take? *(A: 5 seconds).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. Where are you sourcing the thousands of verified medical photographs needed to train this AI accurately?
7. Smartphone cameras vary significantly in quality. How does a blurry photo affect the AI's diagnosis?
8. Does a licensed human doctor verify the AI's results before the patient is notified?
9. What is your statistical false-positive rate?
10. Where is this highly sensitive medical data being stored?

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Legality:** Medical diagnosis is highly illegal without a medical license. What if your AI tells a patient they are healthy, but they actually have cancer?
12. **Regulations:** Have you applied for CDSCO (Indian FDA) approval for medical software devices?
13. **The Pivot:** Instead of diagnosing cancer directly, could the app simply connect the user to a real, licensed dermatologist?
14. **Malpractice:** Who is paying the exorbitant medical malpractice insurance premiums?
15. **The MVP:** How do you test if people actually want this application today without writing a single line of AI code?

## 🎭 Scenario 5: EdTech (The Blockchain Degree Generator)

**You Pitch:** *"I want to put all college degrees on the Blockchain so they cannot be forged."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. What exact problem does this solve for the institution? *(A: It prevents fake certificates).*
2. Who pays for the service? The student or the college? *(A: The college).*
3. Which specific blockchain network are you utilizing? *(A: Ethereum).*
4. Is the ledger public or private? *(A: Public).*
5. Who is responsible for entering the grades into the blockchain? *(A: The faculty).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. Why utilize expensive Blockchain technology instead of issuing a highly secure, digitally signed PDF?
7. What happens if a student loses their crypto wallet private keys? Is their degree inaccessible forever?
8. Who pays the transaction "Gas Fees" every time a new certificate is minted?
9. What happens if a teacher enters the wrong grade? Blockchains are immutable and cannot be edited.
10. Are there vulnerabilities in your smart contracts that could allow hackers to issue fraudulent degrees?

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Adoption:** Colleges already utilize massive ERP systems. How do you intend to force the Principal to abandon their current system and adopt yours?
12. **Regulation:** Do the UGC and AICTE legally recognize blockchain-issued degrees in India?
13. **The MVP:** Can you test this concept manually today with just 10 students using a standard relational database?
14. **Data Laws:** Under Indian data privacy laws, a user possesses the "Right to be Forgotten." How do you delete data from an un-deletable blockchain?
15. **Sales:** Who is the very first Principal you are going to pitch this product to?

## 🎭 Scenario 6: FinTech (The Student Micro-Loan App)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building an app that issues 500 Rupee micro-loans to students who run out of pocket money."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. Where is the capital coming from to issue these loans? *(A: My personal savings).*
2. What is the maximum allowable loan amount? *(A: 1,000 Rupees).*
3. What is the interest rate? *(A: 10% per month).*
4. How quickly is the money transferred to the borrower? *(A: Instantly via UPI).*
5. What is the repayment window? *(A: 30 days).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. College students lack formal income. How do you physically compel them to repay you if they delete the application?
7. What is your projected default rate? (What percentage of students will never repay the loan?)
8. How do you conduct formal KYC (Know Your Customer) verifications on a student?
9. Do you run checks on their CIBIL credit score?
10. What protocol is in place if the student changes their phone number?

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Licensing:** Lending money formally requires strict RBI (Reserve Bank of India) NBFC licenses. How are you operating this legally?
12. **Usury:** Charging 10% per month is considered usury (loan sharking) in many jurisdictions. How do you legally justify this interest rate?
13. **Harassment:** If you contact their parents to recover the funds, can you be sued for harassment or extortion?
14. **The MVP:** Can you test this assumption today by lending 100 Rupees to 5 friends and tracking if they repay you?
15. **Disaster:** What is your contingency plan if 100 students borrow money on Day 1, and zero students repay you on Day 30?

## 🎭 Scenario 7: GreenTech (The Smart Dustbin)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building a smart dustbin that automatically sorts dry and wet waste."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. What technology makes this dustbin 'smart'? *(A: Sensors and AI cameras).*
2. Where does it draw power from? *(A: It plugs directly into a wall outlet).*
3. Is it designed for indoor or outdoor usage? *(A: Indoors).*
4. What are its physical dimensions? *(A: Standard kitchen size).*
5. Who is the primary target buyer? *(A: Smart cities and municipalities).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. How often does the camera lens become obscured by wet garbage, rendering the AI useless?
7. What is the mechanical lifespan of the robotic sorting arm?
8. How do you protect the unit from theft or vandalism if placed on the street?
9. What is the verified accuracy percentage of the sorting algorithm?
10. Does the unit emit foul odors because the mechanical sorting process takes time?

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **Cost ROI:** A smart dustbin costs 50,000 Rupees. A standard plastic one costs 500 Rupees. How do you mathematically prove to the government that they will save money?
12. **Procurement:** Government tenders are notoriously difficult to secure. How will a college student win a municipal contract?
13. **The MVP:** How do you validate that citizens care about automated waste sorting today without actually building a robot?
14. **E-Waste:** Aren't you contributing to electronic waste by putting silicon chips and batteries inside dustbins?
15. **Patents:** Have you secured the hardware patents for the proprietary robotic sorting arm?

## 🎭 Scenario 8: Services / Marketplace (The College Fiverr)

**You Pitch:** *"I am building a freelance marketplace exclusively for our college, where students can hire peers to complete assignments."*

**Simple Questions (Level 1):**
1. What is your revenue model? *(A: We take a 10% commission).*
2. What categories of jobs are permitted? *(A: Coding, homework, graphic design).*
3. Do you hold the funds in a secure escrow account? *(A: Yes).*
4. How do users verify they are legitimate students? *(A: College ID card upload).*
5. Is this platform a website or a mobile application? *(A: A website).*

**Medium Questions (Level 2):**
6. How do you prevent students from connecting on your platform, but processing the payment in cash in the canteen to circumvent your 10% fee?
7. What is the dispute resolution process if a buyer refuses to pay, claiming the submitted work is substandard?
8. How do you mitigate the creation of fake 5-star reviews?
9. Is there a minimum financial threshold required for a freelancer to withdraw their earnings?
10. How do you vet and guarantee the quality of the freelancers on the platform?

**Hard Questions (Level 3):**
11. **The Chicken and Egg:** A marketplace requires both buyers and sellers simultaneously. How do you acquire buyers when there are no freelancers, and vice versa?
12. **Liability:** If a student hires someone to complete their homework, and the professor detects plagiarism, can the college administration suspend you for facilitating academic dishonesty?
13. **Margins:** Payment gateways (like Razorpay) charge a 2% processing fee. If you only take 10%, your profit margin is critically thin. How does the business survive?
14. **The MVP:** Can you validate this marketplace today by acting as a manual broker in a WhatsApp group?
15. **Scale:** How do you successfully expand this highly localized platform to a second college where you have zero network connections?
