# IIC Coordinator Playbook: The Official Classroom Pitch

**ATTENTION ALL 22 IIC COORDINATORS:**
When we speak to classrooms, we represent the **Ministry of Education** and **ACE Engineering College**. We cannot sound unsure, confusing, or "cheap." If we don't explain this powerfully, students will think the IIC is fake or just another useless college club. 

From now on, everyone must use this exact pitch when talking to students.

---

## 🎤 The 60-Second Classroom Pitch Script
*(Memorize this or read it clearly with confidence)*

"Good morning everyone. I am here representing the **Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC)**, an official initiative by the **Ministry of Education, Government of India**, right here at ACE Engineering College.

Right now, everyone in this room is studying to get a job. But the IIC is here to turn you into the people who *create* the jobs. 

We are officially launching the **IIC x ACE Idea Cafe**. 
It doesn’t matter if you are in CSE, Mechanical, or AI. If you have an idea—whether it's an app, a physical product, or a business concept—we want to hear it. 

If you pitch your idea to us, the IIC will help you:
1. Connect with **IIT Alumni (PALS)** for elite mentorship.
2. Build a working prototype.
3. Upload your startup to the Government's **YUKTI Portal** to secure potential funding.

We aren't asking for money, and we aren't asking for perfect business plans. We just want your raw ideas. You can pitch solo, or in a group of up to 5. 

I am dropping the registration link in your class WhatsApp group today. Stop seeking jobs, and start creating them. Thank you."

---

## ❓ How to Answer Student Doubts (FAQ)

If students ask questions or doubt if this is real, use these exact answers:

**Doubt 1: "Is this just another college club activity?"**
**Your Answer:** "No. The IIC is mandated by the Ministry of Education (MoE). The ideas we select are officially uploaded to the Government's YUKTI portal. This is a national-level startup pipeline, not a college club."

**Doubt 2: "I don't have a prototype or a PPT. Can I still join?"**
**Your Answer:** "Yes! A PPT is NOT mandatory. You can just stand up and explain your idea. The Idea Cafe is about the concept, not the presentation."

**Doubt 3: "My idea is very basic, will I get rejected?"**
**Your Answer:** "There are zero domain restrictions. Even if your idea is basic, we have mentors who will help you refine it. Register anyway."

---
**Message from the Convener:** Do not explain the IIC in your own words if you are not confident. Use this script. It makes us look professional, official, and powerful.
