# Event Proposal: Ignite ACE Idea Camp

**To:** The President, Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC)  
**From:** Head of Startup Awareness Program, ACE IIC Students Council  
**Subject:** Proposal and Execution Plan for "Idea Camp" (Idea-Café Activity)  

---

## 1. Event Overview
*   **Event Name:** Ignite ACE: Idea Camp 
*   **Event Type:** IIC Calendar Activity (Quarter 1/2) – Inspiration, Motivation, and Ideation
*   **Target Audience:** 2nd and 3rd Year Engineering Students
*   **Duration:** 90 Minutes
*   **Proposed Date/Time:** 23rd July 2026
*   **Proposed Venue:** Incubation Center

## 2. Objective & Expected Outcomes
The primary objective of this event is to break the ice regarding entrepreneurship, remove the fear of failure, and encourage students to actively brainstorm solutions to real-world problems. 

**Measurable KPIs for IIC Star Rating:**
*   Sensitize at least 50+ students to the startup ecosystem.
*   Generate 15+ multidisciplinary ideas during the session.
*   **Primary Goal:** Successfully onboard and register at least 10+ student ideas onto the **YUKTI (National Innovation Repository)** portal during the event.
*   Identify 2-3 high-potential teams for further mentorship via the **PALS** network.

---

## 3. Resource Requirements (Zero-Budget Pilot)
This first Idea Camp is designed as a **Zero-Budget Pilot Event**. We will utilize existing college infrastructure to maximize impact without any financial cost.

| Resource Required | Purpose | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Seminar Hall / Classroom | A room with a projector and seating for 50 students. | Existing College Facility |
| Whiteboard & Markers | For the core team to write down brainstormed problems. | Existing Classroom Supplies |
| Students' Own Notebooks | Students will use their own notebooks for team ideation instead of sticky notes. | Provided by Students |
| Digital YUKTI QR Code | The QR code will be displayed on the projector screen for students to scan, eliminating printing costs. | Digital Display |

---

## 4. Detailed Agenda (90-Minute Execution Plan)

### Phase 1: Ice Breaking (30 Minutes)
*   **00:00 - 00:10 | Welcome & The Vision:** Introduction by the Head of Awareness. Brief overview of IIC, YUKTI, and PALS. 
*   **00:10 - 00:30 | The "Bad Ideas Only" Exercise:** Students are challenged to pitch the worst, most useless startup idea possible. This removes the fear of judgement and sets a highly energetic, creative tone for the room.

### Phase 2: Problem Finding & Brainstorming (30 Minutes)
*   **00:30 - 00:45 | The Grievance Board:** Students write down daily frustrations (college, transport, daily life) on sticky notes and place them on the wall. 
*   **00:45 - 01:00 | Team Ideation:** Students form teams of 3-4, select one major problem from the wall, and brainstorm a viable technological or business solution. Mentors/Council members walk the floor to assist.

### Phase 3: Pitching & YUKTI Registration (30 Minutes)
*   **01:00 - 01:20 | The 60-Second Pitch:** Each team stands up and delivers a 1-minute pitch of their problem and solution. 
*   **01:20 - 01:30 | YUKTI Onboarding (Call to Action):** Teams with viable ideas are directed to scan the QR codes on the walls and immediately register their ideas on the **YUKTI Portal** before leaving the room. 

---

## 5. Post-Event Action Items
1.  **Documentation:** Photos and attendance sheets will be immediately compiled and uploaded to the IIC portal by the management team.
2.  **Follow-up:** The core team will reach out to the top 3 performing teams to connect them with PALS mentors or the R&D Cell for PoC (Proof of Concept) development.
3.  **Feedback:** Collect feedback via a quick WhatsApp poll in the newly formed "Ignite ACE" community group.
