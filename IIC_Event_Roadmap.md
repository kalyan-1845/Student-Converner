# IIC x ACE: The 6-Week Mega Sprint (IIC 8.0 Finale)

Because the IIC 8.0 Calendar closes on August 31, 2026, we are switching to an **extremely aggressive schedule**. 
You have a team of 22 students. To get maximum points for the college, you need to conduct **2 events per week** for the next 6 weeks. 

Here is the ultimate master plan to execute 12 high-scoring events before the deadline.

---

### 🔥 WEEK 1 (Late July): The Ideation Kickoff
**1. Administrative Win:** Upload the "Startup Awareness" photos/report to the portal immediately. (Self-Driven Activity)
**2. The IIC x ACE Idea Cafe:** Launch the form, collect the ideas, and host the pitching session. (IIC Calendar Activity)

### 🔥 WEEK 2 (Early August): Protection & Design
**3. IPR & Patent Awareness Workshop:** Have a faculty member present the IPR PPT. Very easy to organize, massive points.
**4. "Out of the Box" Design Thinking Workshop:** Gather students in a hall to brainstorm solutions to local problems (traffic, water, college apps). 

### 🔥 WEEK 3 (Mid August): Motivation & Building
**5. "My Story" Founder Session:** Invite a local startup founder or alumni to give a motivational speech.
**6. Prototype Development Bootcamp:** A hands-on session where Idea Cafe students build physical or software models of their ideas.

### 🔥 WEEK 4 (Mid-Late August): Business & Finance
**7. Lean Startup & MVP Workshop:** Teach students how to build a Minimum Viable Product without spending money. 
**8. Angel Investment & VC Funding Session:** Invite a finance professor or guest speaker to explain how startups raise money.

### 🔥 WEEK 5 (August 17 - 23): Celebration & Exposure
**9. World Entrepreneurs Day (August 21):** *MANDATORY CELEBRATION ACTIVITY.* Host a major college-wide celebration, cut a cake, or have a panel discussion.
**10. Incubation Center Field Visit:** Take a bus of 40 students to T-Hub or a local incubator.

### 🔥 WEEK 6 (August 24 - 31): The Grand Finale & Uploads
**11. The Ultimate B-Plan Pitch Competition:** The final pitch day for all the ideas we collected over the last 5 weeks.
**12. YUKTI Portal Submission Drive:** A final, quiet session where you and your 22 team members sit down and officially upload all the winning ideas, business plans, and reports to the MoE YUKTI portal before the midnight deadline on Aug 31st.

---

**💡 CONVENER SURVIVAL TIP:**
Do not try to do all of this yourself. You have 22 student coordinators! 
Divide them into teams of 3. Assign each team to manage **one specific event** (booking the room, taking photos, getting the speaker). Your job is just to oversee them and make sure it gets done!
