# New Google Form: Bootcamp Registration

If you want to send out a new form to get better data before Saturday, you must make the idea questions **REQUIRED** (turn on the "Required" switch on Google Forms for every question). 

Do not let them leave it blank! Here are the exact questions you should put in the new Google Form:

---

### Section 1: Basic Details
1. **Full Name (Team Leader)** *(Required)*
2. **Email ID** *(Required)*
3. **Phone Number / WhatsApp** *(Required)*
4. **Year of Study & Branch** *(Required)*
5. **Team Members (Names & Roll Numbers)** *(Leave blank if Solo)*

### Section 2: The Idea (Mandatory)
*Important: You must make all of these required.*

6. **What category is your startup? (Multiple Choice)** *(Required)*
    *   Software / App (SaaS)
    *   Hardware / Physical Product
    *   Service / Consulting
    *   E-Commerce / Marketplace
    *   I don't know yet

7. **What EXACT problem are you solving?** *(Required - Paragraph)*
    *   *(Hint: Do not say "saving the world". Say exactly what problem exists. E.g., "College students forget to take attendance.")*
    *   *(Note: If you do not have an idea yet, type "I need help finding an idea")*

8. **Who is your first customer?** *(Required - Short Answer)*
    *   *(Hint: Who will actually pay for this? E.g., "College Principals", "Local farmers", "1st Year students")*
    *   *(Note: If you do not have an idea yet, type "I need help")*

9. **What is your solution?** *(Required - Paragraph)*
    *   *(Hint: Explain your product in simple English. No buzzwords.)*
    *   *(Note: If you do not have an idea yet, type "I need help")*

---
**Why this form works:** By adding the rule *"If you do not have an idea yet, type 'I need help'"*, you force the students to admit they are stuck. This means on Saturday, your coordinators will know exactly which tables need to be pushed to brainstorm, and which tables already have ideas!
