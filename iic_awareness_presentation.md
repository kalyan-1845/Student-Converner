**Institution's Innovation Council**

# The Roadmap to Success

We will guide you every step of the way, from a simple idea in your head to a funded startup.

1. **Ideation:** Brainstorming and finding the right problem to solve.
2. **Prototyping:** Building your Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
3. **Mentorship:** Getting feedback from PALS and IIC experts.
4. **Funding:** Pitching to investors and government grants (YUKTI).
5. **Launch:** Taking your product to the market!
