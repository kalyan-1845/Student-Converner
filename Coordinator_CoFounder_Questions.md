# Coordinator Cheat Sheet: The Co-Founder Grill

**Your Role on Saturday:** You are NOT an event organizer today. You are sitting at a table with a founder (the participant). For the next 3 hours, you are their Co-Founder. 
Your job is to help them build their idea, but also to ask them **hard questions** so their idea is strong enough to win the Smart India Hackathon (SIH).

When you sit at your table, use these 4 specific "Grill Questions" to challenge the founder. Do not let them give you vague answers!

---

## 🔪 1. The "Who Cares?" Question
*   **Ask them:** *"If we launch this product today, who is the exact first person who will actually care enough to use it?"*
*   **What to look for:** If the founder says "Everyone will use it," they are wrong. Force them to be specific. (e.g., "1st-year engineering students who need cheap textbooks").

## 🔪 2. The "Google" Question
*   **Ask them:** *"Big companies like Google, Amazon, or Swiggy exist. Why would a customer use our messy, new product instead of theirs?"*
*   **What to look for:** The founder must find one thing that makes them unique. Are they cheaper? Are they faster? Are they hyper-local just for the college campus?

## 🔪 3. The "Worst Case Scenario" Question
*   **Ask them:** *"Let's pretend it is 6 months from now and our startup completely failed. What is the #1 reason it failed?"*
*   **What to look for:** This forces the founder to see the weakness in their own idea. (e.g., "It failed because the hardware was too expensive to build"). Once they admit the weakness, help them fix it!

## 🔪 4. The "No Code" Question (The MVP Test)
*   **Ask them:** *"We have no money and we don't know how to code. How can we test this idea manually by tomorrow morning?"*
*   **What to look for:** If they want to build a marketplace app, tell them to just start a WhatsApp group. If they want to build an AI resume reviewer, tell them to review 5 resumes manually on paper first. Force them to think of the Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

---
**Convener's Note to Coordinators:** Do not be mean, but do not let the founder be lazy. Challenge them, write down their best answers, and prepare them to present their final idea to the room!
