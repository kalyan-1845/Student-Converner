# 🏕️ The "Idea Camp" Blueprint

Hosting your first "Idea Camp" (or Idea-Café) is the perfect first step. 
**The Goal:** Do not ask students to build a company in one day. The goal is simply to get them to stop being afraid of sharing ideas and to register on the YUKTI portal.

Here is your complete guide to running a successful 90-minute Idea Camp.

---

## 1. Pre-Event Setup (What you need)

Keep it casual. If it feels like a boring lecture, students won't speak up.
*   **The Room:** Book a room with round tables if possible, or arrange chairs in a circle. You want people facing each other, not staring at a whiteboard.
*   **Materials Checklist:**
    *   Sticky notes (Post-its) in different colors.
    *   Sharpies or thick markers.
    *   A large whiteboard or chart paper.
    *   **Snacks:** *Crucial.* A few boxes of pizza or just biscuits and tea will double your attendance.
    *   **The YUKTI QR Code:** Print a large QR code linking to the YUKTI registration page and stick it on the walls.

---

## 2. The 90-Minute Event Agenda

### Phase 1: The Icebreaker & "Bad Ideas Only" (30 Mins)
*   **Welcome (5 mins):** Introduce yourself. Keep it high energy. Tell them: *"Today is not about building the next Google. Today is about finding problems that annoy us."*
*   **The "Bad Ideas Only" Game (25 mins):** 
    *   Students are usually terrified that their idea is "stupid".
    *   Tell everyone they have 5 minutes to come up with the **absolute worst, most useless startup idea** they can think of. 
    *   *Example:* A solar-powered flashlight, or a waterproof tea bag.
    *   Have them present it. Everyone will laugh, the ice will break, and the fear of judgement will disappear.

### Phase 2: Problem Finding (30 Mins)
*   Now that they are relaxed, pivot to real problems. 
*   **The Prompt:** Ask them to write down on sticky notes: *"What is one thing about college life, your city, or daily routine that frustrates you deeply?"*
*   Have them stick the notes on the wall. Group similar problems together (e.g., transport issues, food issues, study issues).
*   Split the room into teams of 3-4 and assign each team one of the problem groups to brainstorm a tech or business solution.

### Phase 3: The 60-Second Pitch & YUKTI (30 Mins)
*   **The Pitch:** Give each team 60 seconds to stand up and pitch their solution to the room. 
*   **The Magic Trick:** When a team pitches a good idea, act extremely impressed. Say: *"That is actually a brilliant idea. You need to protect that."*
*   **The Call to Action (The most important part):** Point to the QR code on the wall. Tell them: *"Take out your phones right now, scan this, and put your idea into the YUKTI portal so it's officially registered under your name."* Walk around and make sure they actually do it.

---

## 3. Post-Event (The Follow-Up)

*   **The WhatsApp Group:** Make sure everyone who attended is in your "Ignite ACE" (or whatever you named your team) WhatsApp group.
*   **The PALS Connection:** If you noticed 2 or 3 students who were incredibly passionate or had amazing tech ideas, pull them aside after the event. Tell them about **PALS** and that you want to connect them with mentors. They will feel like VIPs.
*   **Report to IIC:** Take photos during the event! Send the photos and the number of students who registered on YUKTI to Dr. Murali Sir or the IIC President to boost your college's Star Rating.

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Play light, energetic background music (lo-fi or instrumental) when they are brainstorming. Silence in a room makes people nervous to talk!
