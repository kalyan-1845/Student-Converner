# IIC Coordinator Playbook: How to Fix "Cheap" Ideas

**ATTENTION TEAM:** 
Many students registering for the Idea Cafe will bring very basic, poorly explained, or "cheap" sounding ideas. They will also doubt their own ideas (saying things like, *"Does this already exist?"*). 

**It is our job to help them fix their pitches before they present.** 
If a student explains their idea poorly, use this guide to help them sound professional.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Fix the "Cheap" Explanation
Most students explain their ideas by just naming the technology: *"I want to build an AI chatbot for college."* This sounds cheap. 

**How to fix it:** Force them to focus on the **PROBLEM**, not just the product. 
Tell the student to restructure their pitch using these 3 sentences:
1. **The Problem:** *"Currently, students waste 2 hours a day trying to find their syllabus..."*
2. **The Solution:** *"My idea is an AI chatbot that instantly gives them the exact page they need."*
3. **The Impact:** *"This saves time and helps students study faster."*

Suddenly, their "cheap" idea sounds like a professional startup pitch!

---

## 🛠️ Step 2: Fix the "Does this already exist?" Doubt
Many students will doubt their own idea because they think someone else (like Google or a big company) already did it. 

**How to answer them:**
Tell the student: *"Swiggy and Zomato both exist. Flipkart and Amazon both exist. It does not matter if the idea already exists. What matters is: How is yours different? Is it cheaper? Is it specifically designed for our local area or our college?"*

If they can find even ONE small thing that makes their idea unique, it is a valid startup.

---

## 🛠️ Step 3: Tell them NOT to fake it
If a student's idea is very basic, tell them **not to use complex buzzwords** to make it sound better. 
If they just want to open a unique food stall, tell them to pitch it as a food stall! A solid, simple business plan is much better than a fake, confusing "AI Blockchain" idea.

**Remember:** Our job in the Idea Cafe is to listen to their raw, messy ideas and help them clean them up so they look good on the YUKTI portal!
