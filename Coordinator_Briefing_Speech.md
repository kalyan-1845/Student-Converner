# Convener's Briefing: The Coordinator Meeting

*(Read this speech to your 40 coordinators at tomorrow's meeting to explain their new roles)*

**"Listen up, team.** 

This Saturday's event is going to be completely different from anything we have done before. We have exactly 10 startup ideas pitching, and there are 40 of us in this room. 

Because we have the numbers advantage, we are switching to a **'Consulting Firm'** model. 

You are no longer just event organizers taking attendance. For the entire 3 hours on Saturday, you are going to act as **Co-Founders and Consultants**. 

Here is how it will work:
We will have 10 tables set up in Room 2408. One founder sits at each table. I am assigning exactly **4 of you** to sit at the table with them. 

Your job is NOT to just sit there and nod your head while they talk. Your job is to rip their idea apart and put it back together so it is strong enough to win the Smart India Hackathon (SIH). 

I am sending a 'Cheat Sheet' to our WhatsApp group right now. It has 4 specific questions on it—like 'How do you test this without writing code?' and 'Why would I use this instead of Google?' 

You will use those 4 questions to grill the founder at your table. Make them think. Force them to write down better answers. 

I am stepping back on Saturday. You 40 are taking the lead. I expect you to act like professional startup mentors. Let's make this the best event ACE Engineering College has ever seen."
